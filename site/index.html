Security & Privacy

Your Data Stays 100% Private

We take privacy and performance seriously. Here is our unwavering promise to you.

🛡️

Zero Data Collection

The extension never reads, stores, or transmits your spreadsheet cells, formulas, or personal information.

🔒

Zero External Requests

Operates 100% offline. No telemetry, no third-party tracking scripts, and no analytics beacons whatsoever.

💾

Local Browser Storage

Settings like your favorite color and opacity are saved locally via chrome.storage.local on your computer.

⚡

Manifest V3 Compliant

Built on Google Chrome's newest Manifest V3 standard with minimal, transparent permissions scoped only to Google Sheets.
